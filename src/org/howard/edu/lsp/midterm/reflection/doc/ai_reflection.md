# Question 4 — Personal Reflection on AI in Software Development
Throughout this semester, you have had opportunities to use artificial intelligence (AI) tools while studying object-oriented
programming and completing coursework. Reflect on your own experience in this course.

## Benefits: Explain how AI has helped your learning or software development this semester. Include at least one specific example from this course.

AI has helped me increase the efficiency of my development throughout this semester, by filling on implementation when a structured high level description of the task is given. For example in this class when providing the necessary context (diagrams, question description, and immediate callouts/assumptions) I've discovered that AI tools can produce a good enough runnable draft for me to look over and improve upon while cutting out the hours it would've taken me to produce the code.

## Challenges: Describe a situation in which AI was incorrect, misleading, unhelpful, or made learning more difficult. Explain what you learned from that experience. If you did not encounter such a situation, explain a limitation or risk you have observed and how you address it.

I encountered a situation in which AI made learning more difficult while completing tasks for a research team this semester. I was given a very rough description of a task I was to implement without any personal background knowledge, and thus had to rely on AI to fill in the gaps. However, because I didn't have much to provide the tools in how I specifically wanted to approach my implementation, it filled in based off of what it understood my problem to be, and I had no way of double checking that I was on the right path as I wasn't sure of how to read the intermediary results building the project, only how the final output should look/be structured.

## Moving Forward: Explain how you intend to use AI in future courses or professional software development while continuing to develop your own technical skills.

In the future I intend to use AI in Software Development as a tool of assistance. AI is a great help as a research assistant to gather more information about a topic you might be lacking in. And from there after digesting and understanding the information, you can begin to formulate design decisions on how exactly you want your project to be structured. AI can also be used to help plan the implementation of a project as well. However, its response will only be as good as the context you give it to work on, so I still intend to do the heavy lifitng in sorting out what requirements I need, packages to import, design decisions, etc.
